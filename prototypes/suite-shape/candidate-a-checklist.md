# Suite checklist: Sunlit Flock (Kitchen 20)

Walked by the model when no code sandbox exists. Count against the Oracle where
present; with no Oracle, use card knowledge and flag every verdict best-effort.
Mark each line red or green with the counted evidence — never a bare tick.

- [ ] `legality.size` — Deck has exactly 20 cards.
- [ ] `legality.singleton` — No nonland card appears more than 1 time(s).
- [ ] `legality.mono_color` — Deck uses at most 1 color and 0 multicolor cards.
- [ ] `legality.rare_count` — Exactly 1 rare, nothing above rare.
- [ ] `legality.land_count` — Between 8 and 9 lands.
- [ ] `legality.nonbasic_lands` — Only nonbasic lands on the allowed list (Uncharted Haven).
- [ ] `legality.evergreen` — Every keyword on every card is on the evergreen list.
- [ ] `availability.in_collection` — Every card (at its count) exists in the Collection.
- [ ] `manabase.color_coverage` — Lands can produce every color the spells ask for.
- [ ] `curve.average` — Average nonland mana value is at most 3.0.
- [ ] `curve.early_plays` — At least 4 nonland cards at mana value 2 or less.
- [ ] `quota.creature` — At least 8 cards tagged creature.
- [ ] `quota.theme` — At least 6 cards tagged theme.
- [ ] `quota.removal` — At least 2 cards tagged removal.
- [ ] `quota.draw` — At least 1 cards tagged draw.
- [ ] `quota.wincon` — At least 1 cards tagged wincon.
- [ ] `brief.cmc_max` — No card above mana value 4.
- [ ] `consistency.opening_lands` — P(at least 2 lands in a 7-card opening hand) is at least 0.85.
